Terms of Use

1. What Mjadala is

Mjadala is a small chat app where people discuss political and social issues in rooms. It is owned and run by Mark Sixstars. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

2. Who can use it

You must be at least 18 years old. You use a nickname, not your real name. One person should have one account. We may remove accounts we believe are duplicates or fake.

3. Rules

Argue with facts, not insults. You must not post:

  • Hate speech, or content that attacks people for their tribe, ethnicity, religion, gender, disability or similar
  • Threats, incitement to violence, or harassment of any person
  • Other people's private information (home address, phone number, ID, photos) without their consent
  • Information you know is false, presented as fact. When you make a claim, bring sources
  • Sexual content, or anything involving the abuse of children
  • Spam, scams, or anything else that is illegal in Kenya

4. Moderation

Anyone can report a message with the Report link. When several different people report the same message, it is hidden automatically. Admins and moderators may delete messages or rooms, restrict or ban accounts, and remove content that breaks these rules or the law, with or without warning.

5. Your content

You keep ownership of what you write. By posting, you allow Mjadala to store it and show it to other signed-in users in the room. You are responsible for what you post. Opinions in the rooms belong to the people who wrote them, not to Mjadala.

6. No guarantees

Mjadala is provided as is, and it may have errors or go offline. It is not a news service, and it is not a scientific opinion poll: the people who join are not a representative sample of Kenya.

7. Changes and law

We may update these terms, and the date above will change. If you keep using Mjadala after a change, you accept it. These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Your nickname, which other users can see
  • A login identifier made from your nickname. We do not ask for your real name, email or phone number at this time
  • Your password, which our login provider stores in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot read it
  • What you create: messages, rooms and reports you make
  • Technical data such as IP address and device type, which our hosting and database providers process to run the service

How we use it

To run the app, keep it safe, handle reports, and enforce the rules. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see what

Your nickname and messages are visible to other signed-in users in the room. Admins and moderators can see reports. We may share information with authorities when the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our service providers (Supabase for the database and logins, Vercel for hosting). Their servers may be outside Kenya.

Your browser

The app keeps your login session in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Ask the owner, Mark Sixstars, through the contact details published on this site. We will respond within the time the law allows.

Keeping data

We keep your data while your account exists, and for as long as needed to keep the service safe or to meet the law. No online service is completely secure, so choose a password you do not use anywhere else.

If we later add features such as phone number verification, we will update this page and tell you before we collect any new kind of data.
