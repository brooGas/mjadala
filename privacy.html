Mjadala Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Mjadala uses information you provide to operate your account and social features.

Information we collect

This may include your name, email address, profile details, content you post, interactions, and technical information needed to secure and operate the service.

How information is used

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, display content and profiles, provide social features, respond to reports, and improve reliability.

Email verification

When creating an account, Mjadala may send a one-time verification code to your email address. We use the result to verify control of that email address.

Sharing and service providers

Mjadala may process data through infrastructure providers such as Supabase to provide authentication, database, and storage services. We do not ask you to publish passwords or verification codes.

Your choices

Keep your login credentials private. You may request help with account access or privacy questions through Mjadala's designated support channel when available.

Security and changes

We use access controls and service-provider security features, but no online service can promise absolute security. This policy may be updated as Mjadala evolves; material updates should be communicated in the app.
